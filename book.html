FAQs
What happens during the site visit?

A senior MIBO designer visits your home, measures the space, understands your lifestyle and aesthetic, and creates a tailored design brief with material shortlists and a transparent project estimate.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — full refund if you cancel at least 48 hours before your visit. Partial refunds for later cancellations are handled by our admin team on a case-by-case basis. If you proceed with your project, the visit fee is credited in full.

Why is there a visit fee?

The site visit fee ensures serious, committed clients and covers our designer's time, travel, and the detailed brief they prepare. It is fully credited back to you when your project begins — so it's effectively free if you proceed.

How are payments processed?

We use Razorpay — India's leading payment gateway. We accept UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), all major debit/credit cards, and net banking. Your payment is fully secured and encrypted.
