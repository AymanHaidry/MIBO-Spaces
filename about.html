Our Journey

Built one home at a time

2012

The First Project

MIBO began as a small interior consultancy in Patna, Bihar, with a single belief: that homes in Tier 2 and Tier 3 India deserved world-class design. Our first project — a 1,200 sq ft apartment — became our proof of concept.

2015

MIBOGypsum is Born

We realised that beautiful interiors required beautiful structures. We launched our in-house drywall and gypsum division, giving us complete control over walls, ceilings, and partitions — end to end.

2018

Tiles & Material Studio

We expanded into curated material sourcing — ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and glass tile collections — allowing clients to touch and choose their surfaces at our studio before a single tile was laid.

2020

Going All-India

We extended our site visit model nationally. With remote consultation slots every Friday and Saturday, MIBO now serves homes across India — from Mumbai penthouses to Bangalore villas to new builds in Jharkhand.

2024

250+ Homes Transformed

A decade of designing homes one story at a time. 250 completed projects. 12 states. Countless families who now wake up to spaces that feel exactly like themselves.

Today

Still Growing, Still Residential

We remain resolutely residential. Every new service, every new city — always for the home. Because home is the most important design project any of us will ever live in.
